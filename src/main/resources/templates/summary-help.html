How the summary works

← Back to the summary

The table

Each meal is counted once. It is followed by pain if an episode started at the time of the meal or up to the lookback window after it (set in Settings, 6 hours unless you change it). It is over if its fat total is above your limit; a meal exactly at the limit counts as within.

A meal from the last few hours with no episode yet is left out until its window ends, because pain could still follow. Deleted meals and episodes are left out.

Median time to pain

For every meal followed by pain, the app takes the time from the meal to the first episode after it. The median is the middle of those times: half were shorter, half longer.

Why small counts are unreliable

With few episodes, one more or one fewer changes a percentage a lot: 1 of 2 meals is 50%, 2 of 2 is 100%. That is why percentages stay hidden until you have logged 5 episodes, and even after that a difference between the rows can be chance.

One episode can follow several meals, so it can be counted more than once. Things other than fat, such as meal size or timing, can also matter.

This is a record to discuss with your doctor, not a diagnosis.
